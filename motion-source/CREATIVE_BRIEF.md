# CREATIVE_BRIEF — Prospectify premium motion piece

> This is the brief, **not** the storyboard. No shots, timings or scripts are fixed here.
> Facts come only from `PRODUCT_TRUTH.md`. The look comes only from `brand/BRAND_SOURCE.md` and `ui/`.

## Objective
Make an AI-website freelancer feel, in under a minute, that **finding the client is now the easy part**, then
start the free trial (3 leads, no card).

## Audience
Freelancers and side-hustlers who can already build a website with AI / no-code (Lovable, Bolt, Base44,
Framer, Webflow…).
- They are skilled with the tools and stuck on demand: no clients, cold DMs that go nowhere, hours spent
  scrolling Google Maps.
- They are fluent in short-form video and allergic to hype.

## Pain (true, specific)
- Building got fast; finding someone to pay you didn't.
- Searching by hand is slow and random.
- You don't know who actually needs a site, what to say to them, or how to follow up.

## Emotional target
From **scattered → certain**: the calm of having the right lead, the right message and the right prompt in hand.
- Confidence, not hype.
- The feeling of a tool that is already doing the boring part.

## Single-minded proposition
**Find clients. Sell AI websites.** Prospectify finds the local business that needs a website, scores it, and hands
you the message and the build prompt.

## Proof points allowed (from PRODUCT_TRUTH only)
- Real local businesses, worldwide, verified on Google Maps.
- No website / social-only / weak site.
- Opportunity score 0–100.
- Outreach in WhatsApp, email or phone script.
- An AI prompt tailored to your builder.
- A tracker from Contacted to Sold.
- 3 free leads, no card. $25 / $45 plans, if pricing is shown at all.

## Motion principle — CHAOS → SIGNAL → SCAN → LOCK → READY
1. **CHAOS:** the noisy world of local businesses: a dense map, too many pins, no idea where to start. Dense,
   restless, low contrast.
2. **SIGNAL:** one business surfaces: no website, strong reputation. The single accent appears for the first time.
3. **SCAN:** Prospectify reads the business: website status, rating, reviews, pain points. The score counts to
   its value.
4. **LOCK:** the lead card locks into place (the real UI component). Camera settles; this is the sharpest, stillest
   frame.
5. **READY:** message, AI prompt and tracker resolve around it. Then the brand and the CTA.

Calm and stillness increase as the piece moves from CHAOS to LOCK. The edit should *feel* like noise becoming signal.

## Brand rules
- Real logo files only (`brand/prospectify-logo-site.png`). Never redraw, approximate or recolour it.
- Production tokens:
  - bg `#09090b`, surfaces `#111114` / `#16161a`;
  - text `#f5f5f7` / `#a1a1aa`;
  - **one** accent `#f42562`, and the brand gradient only for hero moments (CTA, score, progress).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** (700–800 headlines, tight negative tracking; uppercase tracked accent eyebrows).
- Use the site's own eases where they fit: `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` ease-out, `cubic-bezier(.34,1.4,.5,1)` spring.
- Palette balance: about 85 % near-black, 10 % white/grey structure, 5 % accent.
- Builder logos: the official files from `builders/`, untouched and never tinted pink. Always pair them with the
  "Trademarks belong to their owners. No affiliation implied." note.

## Craft bar (from `references/REFERENCE_SOURCES.md`)
- **UI as protagonist:** tight crops of the real UI, one action per beat, blur-whips between beats, sharp holds
  for reading.
- **One brand object:** a single signal / scan element that carries the concept from CHAOS to LOCK. Type is placed
  in space with depth of field, not animated letter by letter everywhere.
- **Sound as structure:**
  - each UI event gets its own designed sound;
  - one near-silence before the LOCK;
  - the voice-over leads the mix, and music is ducked around it.
- Delivery: 60 fps, vertical 1080×1920 master, 4K deliverable via scale 2. Captions must be safe for sound-off
  viewing.

## Anti-AI-aesthetic (forbidden)
- Purple-blue "AI" gradients, glowing brains, robot hands, sparkle-storms, holographic HUD clutter.
- Generic 3D blobs or abstract shapes standing in for the product.
- Stock-photo people, fake laptops with invented UIs, fake dashboards with invented numbers.
- Serif "signature" italic display type. The founder rejected it.
- Letter-flip / glitch on every line. Motion must earn its place.
- Fake social proof: testimonials, user counts, revenue screenshots, "trusted by" logos.
- Emojis as design elements, and confetti beyond one restrained sale moment.

## Mandatory
- Every on-screen fact must trace back to `PRODUCT_TRUTH.md`.
- Demo businesses look like demo data: plausible, never presented as customers.
- End frame: real logo, "Start for free" (or "Start free — 3 leads, no card"), prospectify.net.

## Open inputs from the founder (optional, but they raise the ceiling)
- The original vector logo (SVG / Figma).
- A demo account, staging URL or screen recordings of the in-app screens.
- The Figma file of the app, if one exists. None was found or invented.
- A voice-over language decision (EN, FR or both) and final CTA wording.
