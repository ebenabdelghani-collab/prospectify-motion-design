# Final creative strategy — Prospectify

**One idea.**
- Building is fast. Finding the right client is slow.
- The film starts in the viewer's world and lets them feel the slow part.
- Then Prospectify turns **noise into signal**: CHAOS → SIGNAL → SCAN → LOCK → READY.

**Viewer.**
- 18 to 30 years old. They already build websites with AI (Lovable, Claude, Bolt, Base44…).
- Their friction: who to sell to, which businesses are worth it, what to say, what to build.

**Target reaction.** "I know how to build the site. This handles the part I was missing."

## Conversion architecture (each act opens the next question)
| Act | Question it opens | Answer it delivers |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Why is finding clients the hard part? | The blinking caret, then a clock running to 1:58 AM |
| Manual | How long does it take by hand? | 12 tabs, other businesses flicking past, **0 pitches sent** |
| Insight | Which one would *you* pitch? | B, every time. **Demand first. Website problem second.** A takeaway worth saving, before any ad |
| Scale | How do you do that 50 times? | You can't. Visual noise peaks, then the frame freezes |
| Reveal | What changes this? | One coral signal threads the field and locks; the Prospectify mark resolves *from the lock* |
| Search / Why | Does it just list businesses? | It ranks them by opportunity score and **tells you why** |
| Ready | What does it prepare? | Contact, angle, outreach → READY (3 / 4 ready) |
| Prompt | What's the 4th? | **The website prompt for that exact business.** The second reveal, slowed down |
| Builder | Where do I build it? | Your choice: official Lovable / Claude / Bolt / Base44 marks |
| Build / Pitch | Does it really become a site? | The prompt's sections become the page's regions; the site turns into the pitch |
| Sell / Track | Then what? | Mark as sold → Record a sale → analytics |
| Loop | Is this a system? | Find → Understand → Contact → Build → Sell → Track → **Repeat** |
| CTA | Is it risky to try? | Start free · 3 real leads · no card |

## Decisions that protect trust
- Every product screen uses production strings and fields; no invented features. See `PRODUCT_TRUTH.md`.
- Demo businesses are fictional, and every product scene carries a **DEMO DATA** tag.
- No testimonials, user counts, revenue claims or promises.
- The one sale ($750) is typed by the user in the film. It matches the real "e.g. 750" placeholder. It is an illustration, not a result.
- Builder marks are the official files, presented as a choice, with "Trademarks belong to their owners. No affiliation implied."
- **Colour is withheld until the reveal.**
  - Acts 1–4 are strictly neutral: white emphasis, no brand accent.
  - The first coral pixel on screen is the Prospectify signal.
  - From then on, colour *means* Prospectify.
