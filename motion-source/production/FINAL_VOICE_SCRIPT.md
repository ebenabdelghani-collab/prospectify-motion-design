## Changes from the canonical territory, and why
- **"No website doesn't automatically mean good client."**
  - The quotation marks were dropped so the line is spoken naturally.
  - On screen it reads "“No website” ≠ good client."
  - This is consistent with the product. The finder covers `none`, `social_only` and `weak` sites, and scores the opportunity on reputation and need.
- **"But finding someone worth pitching it to?"** The "it" makes the line flow on from line 1.
- **"Open their website."** "Their" sounds more spoken than "the".
- **"Ready."** stands alone after the three READY modules. The on-screen counter reads "3 / 4 ready", which sets up the prompt reveal.
