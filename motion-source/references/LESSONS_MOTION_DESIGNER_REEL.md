# Lessons — "made by a motion designer" reference (Instagram reel Dd_8M34ysiN)

The founder's benchmark for "motion designer, not AI motion designer". A 39.5 s product ad on a
laptop screen. The reel itself is not stored here (rights); these are the craft lessons.

## What makes it read as hand-made
- **Two acts, two worlds.** A dark "problem" act (navy grid, glow, vignette) and a warm "solution"
  act (cream and a soft orange sun). A **liquid splat** turns one into the other, not a cut.
- **Type is the main actor.** Heavy grotesk, tight tracking, one word or a short line at a time,
  with one accent colour. Words rise, un-blur, settle, and **explode in 3D** when the idea is done.
- **Hits are physical:** an RGB chromatic split and a micro skew on the beat, decaying in about 10
  frames. Use it on 3–5 moments, never constantly.
- **Real UI fragments, not screenshots:** tab chips, a search bar that types, notification cards,
  rolling numbers. Each one is animated in, used, and thrown away.
- **Rhythm "over here / over there / everywhere":** a three-beat list, each beat with its own
  visual, the third one escalating (a 3D ring of cards).
- **Motion blur only on fast moves**, real easing (fast-lock, heavy), never linear.
- **The end is a tagline couplet plus a CTA.**

## Acquisition structure (founder rule, Lovable-style)
- The **subject is the process** the viewer already knows (find a business, know what to say,
  build the site, send it). The product is **the missing piece inside it**, named late.
- The viewer thinks "I know all this, I only lack this tool".
- Lead with recognisable context (maps, search, tabs, late night), not with the logo.

## Voice direction
- Every line has a **tone** (tired/tense at night, bright coach in the day, confident payoff).
- Lean on the key word of each line (pitch accent and level), and keep one voice for the whole film.
- In this repo: `scripts/playbook/voice_script.py` (EMPH / TONE) and a WORLD-vocoder pass in
  `scripts/playbook/build_voice.py`. Visual cues are timed to real word timings.
